dodojo-arcade-game街机反应Arcade

一根手指就能玩的跳跃、飞行、躲避、接东西、打砖块、节奏和跑酷：三秒学会一个动作，固定步长的循环 60 帧不卡，死得服气，重开不到一秒，最高分跟着你走。One-thumb jumpers, flyers, dodgers, catchers, brick breakers, rhythm games and runners: one verb learned in three seconds, a fixed-step loop at 60 fps, deaths that feel fair, restart in under a second, and a best score that follows you.

npx skills add dodojo-AI/dodojo-skills --skill dodojo-arcade-game
1喂给它Give it
Make me a quick one-thumb arcade game for my commute: short runs, gets harder, and keeps my best score.
输入Input
想要什么样的动作和场景、一局多长；连上 dodojo 时，还有你生活里的城市、宠物The action and the setting, how long a run lasts; plus a city or a pet from your own life in dodojo when connected
产出Output
一页随时来一局的小游戏：场景在最上面，分数在角落，一排按钮，玩法和纪录放在下面A game for one more run: the scene on top with the score in a corner and a row of controls, with how to play and records below
2它怎么做How it works
  1. 1
    找到那一个动作Find the verb一个动作（跳、转向、接、瞄准、踩点），高手和新手差在哪，再配一个给它理由的场景one action (jump, steer, catch, aim, tap on the beat), what the person masters over many runs, and a setting that gives it a reason.
  2. 2
    场景放在最上面Lay it out with the playfield first场景带着分数在上面，下面一细排按钮，再往下是玩法、纪录和这个世界的来历the scene on top with the score in it, a slim row of controls under it, then how to play, records and a note on the world.
  3. 3
    从游戏的世界来设计Design from the game's world一个画面构想，几种在高速下一眼分得清的颜色和形状，先写成方案再写代码one graphic idea, a small palette that reads at speed, shapes a glance can tell apart, written as a plan before any code.
  4. 4
    做循环和操作Build the loop and the controlsrequestAnimationFrame 上的固定步长模拟，输入缓冲到下一步，拇指、鼠标、键盘都顺手a fixed-step simulation on requestAnimationFrame, input buffered to the next step, controls made for a thumb, a mouse and a keyboard.
  5. 5
    手感好、死得服气Make it feel good and fair反馈和事情大小相称，判定比画面宽容，前一分钟慢慢加难，死因看得见，一秒内重开juice in proportion, hitboxes kinder than sprites, a ramp over the first minute, deaths that show their cause, restart in under a second.
  6. 6
    把最高分存住Keep the best最高分、玩了几局和设置都存在页面里；页面切走就暂停the best score, the runs played and the settings kept with the page; a run paused when the page is hidden.
  7. 7
    跑完再存Play it, then save dodojo机器人在 Node 里跑几百个种子，人用触屏、鼠标、键盘玩，降速测帧时间，手机宽度截图后存成 dodojo 笔记a bot and a person playing many runs, frame times measured, every control tried, screenshots at phone width, then a dodojo Note.

步骤取自 SKILL.md 的 Workflow 一节，skill 改了，这里跟着变。金色步骤是和 dodojo 打交道的地方。Steps are read from the Workflow section of SKILL.md, so this page changes with the skill. Gold steps are where dodojo comes in.
