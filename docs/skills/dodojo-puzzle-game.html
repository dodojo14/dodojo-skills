2它怎么做How it works
  1. 1
    先把谜题定下来Pin down the puzzle哪一种、规则写清、手机上放得下的尺寸、每日一题还是关卡包、一道多久the kind, the exact rules, the size that fits a phone, one puzzle a day or a pack of levels, and how long one takes.
  2. 2
    保证每道题公平Make puzzles that are fair手做或按种子生成，每道都过求解器：有解、该唯一的唯一、按需要的推理定难度hand-made or generated from a seed, and every one checked by a solver: solvable, unique where logic should decide, graded by the reasoning it needs.
  3. 3
    排好关卡Shape the levels第一关不用文字就能教会规则，一个新招一关，难度上升中间留喘口气的关the first teaches the rule without words, each new idea gets its own level, difficulty climbs with breathers between.
  4. 4
    谜题放在最上面Lay it out with the puzzle first细细一条状态、尽量大的格子、紧贴着的工具，然后才是规则、关卡和纪录a slim strip, the grid as large as the column allows, the tools under it, then rules, levels and records.
  5. 5
    让人想得舒服Make thinking comfortable标记和笔记、无限撤销、错误只在想看时才显示，提示讲清下一步的理由marks and notes, unlimited undo, mistakes shown only when asked, hints that explain the next step.
  6. 6
    把进度存住Keep the progress每个标记、每道做完的题都存在页面里，换台设备打开还在原处every mark and every finished level kept with the page, so it reopens exactly where the person left it.
  7. 7
    验完再存Solve it, then save dodojo每关和每个种子都过求解器，用触屏、鼠标、键盘亲手解几关，手机宽度截图后存成 dodojo 笔记every level run through the solver, a few solved by hand with touch, mouse and keyboard, screenshots at phone width, then a dodojo Note.

Low Tide picture logic

Picture-logic puzzles from the shore: 12 levels from a 5 × 5 warm-up to 10 × 10, plus a new puzzle every day under Today.

Fill cells so each row and column shows its runs of filled cells in order, and a picture appears; the bulb explains the next step.

Every puzzle has exactly one solution and needs no guessing; your marks are kept, so you can stop and come back.
