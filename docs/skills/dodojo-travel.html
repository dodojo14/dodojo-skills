dodojo-travel旅行页Trip page

一日游、城市行程、自驾、吃喝路线：按出行当天查营业时间、门票和交通，排出不走回头路的路线，配真实照片和地图。Day trips, city itineraries, road trips and food crawls: hours, tickets and transport checked for the actual dates, a route that never backtracks, real photos and a map.

npx skills add dodojo-AI/dodojo-skills --skill dodojo-travel
示例Examples
1喂给它Give it
I'm in Lisbon on Saturday, December 12, staying near Rossio. Plan me a day on foot and by tram: Alfama, the castle, Belém and the pastéis de nata. Make it a page I can follow on my phone.
输入Input
目的地、日期、住在哪；连上 dodojo 时，还有你存过的订单和收藏Where, when and the base; plus bookings and saved places in dodojo when connected
产出Output
一张路上能用的行程页：地图、时间线、门票、清单、总花费A page for the road: map, timeline, tickets, checklist and totals
2它怎么做How it works
  1. 1
    从已知出发Start from what is known dodojo先在 dodojo 里搜订单、收藏和以前的游记，再确定日期、住处、同行人和节奏search the person's dodojo memories for bookings, saved places and earlier trips, then pin down dates, base, party and pace.
  2. 2
    按当天逐个核实Check every place for the actual dates营业时间、闭馆日、节假日、门票、交通、日落和天气，都留来源和查询日期hours, closures, holidays, tickets, transport, light and weather, each with its source and the date checked.
  3. 3
    排能走通的路线Plan routes that work按片区聚类，限时入场优先，一天三到五个点，留余量和雨天方案cluster each day by area, timed entries first, three to five stops, buffers and a rain plan.
  4. 4
    找真实照片Find real photos每个重点地方一张，从 Wikimedia Commons 下载，放在页面旁边并署名one per key place, downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, kept beside the page and credited.
  5. 5
    从地方本身来设计Design from the place配色、字体和细节取自目的地，先写成方案再排版colors, type and details taken from the destination, written as a plan before any layout.
  6. 6
    为路上搭页面Build the page for the road首屏一眼看清，每天一张地图、每站一张卡片、吃什么、清单和花费，全在一个文件里an orienting first screen, then each day's map, a card per stop, food, a checklist and totals, in one file.
  7. 7
    检查后存起来Check and save dodojo手机宽度、深浅色都看，链接和合计逐个核对；存成 dodojo 笔记，要点写进正文phone width in both themes, every link and total checked, then saved as a dodojo Note with the key facts in Markdown.

Lisbon Saturday, Alfama to Belém

One day in Lisbon on Saturday 12 December 2026, from a base near Rossio, on foot and by tram 28E and 15E: castle at nine, Alfama by noon, Belém at sunset (17:15). Tickets and transport come to 64,75 € for one adult; book the Jerónimos 15:00 and Torre de Belém 16:30 entries before the day, because they often sell out.

The day, stop by stop
  • 08:23 Tram 28E from Martim Moniz (6 min walk from Rossio) to Lg. Portas Sol, about 20 min
  • 08:45 Portas do Sol: viewpoint over Alfama, first pastel de nata at Manteigaria, Largo Portas do Sol 90 (08:00–22:00)
  • 09:15 Castelo de São Jorge: 09:00–18:00, last entry 17:30; 17 €
  • 11:30 Largo do Chafariz de Dentro, after 45 min in Alfama's lanes (rain option: Museu do Fado, 10:00–18:00, 5 €)
  • 11:50 Sé de Lisboa: Monday–Saturday 10:00–18:00; 7 € for the choir, tower balcony and Treasury
  • 12:20 Lunch at Prado, Travessa das Pedras Negras 2; book by phone, +351 21 053 4649
  • 13:47 Tram 15E from Pç. Comércio to Mosteiro Jerónimos, about 35 min
  • 14:25 Pastéis de Belém, Rua de Belém 84–92: daily 08:00–21:00; 1,60 € each
  • 15:00 Mosteiro dos Jerónimos cloister: timed entry, 18 €; last entry 17:00, church free (Saturday 10:30–17:00)
  • 16:30 Torre de Belém: timed entry, 15 €; sunset 17:15 from the terrace, closes 17:30
  • 17:40 Tram 15E from Lg. Princesa back to Pç. Figueira, about 35 min
  • 18:20 A Ginjinha, Largo de São Domingos 8, then Rossio's Christmas market (15 November – 21 December 2026)
  • 19:30 Dinner at Casa do Alentejo, Rua das Portas de Santo Antão 58 (restaurant 19:00–23:00); book online
Book before the day
  • Castle ticket (not timed; skips the ticket-office queue)
  • Jerónimos cloister, 15:00 entry
  • Torre de Belém, 16:30 entry
  • Prado lunch for 12:20, by phone: +351 21 053 4649
  • Casa do Alentejo dinner for 19:30

Food comes on top: pastel de Belém 1,60 €, carne de porco à alentejana 18,00 € at Casa do Alentejo (October 2025 menu).

Getting around

Buy a navegante occasional card (0,50 €) at a Metro machine and load the 24-hour Carris/Metro ticket (7,25 €); it covers every tram, bus and Metro ride from the first tap. Zapping is 1,72 € a Carris ride; paying on the tram is 3,30 €. Check Carris service changes that morning. Emergency number: 112.

Weather and light

Usual December, not a forecast: about 15 °C by day and 9 °C at night, rain on about 10 days of the month (IPMA climate normal 1991–2020). Sunrise 07:45, sunset 17:15, dark by 17:45 (timeanddate). Check the IPMA forecast from about ten days before.
