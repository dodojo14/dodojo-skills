dodojo-game棋牌对战Board game

五子棋、象棋、围棋、牌类和原创桌游：游戏放在最上面，点下去马上有手感和声音，电脑对手分档，两人同屏或团队空间里各用各的手机，每一步都存着，画面取自棋盘棋子的材质。Gomoku, chess, go, card games and original tabletop games: the game on top, input and sound that answer at once, a computer opponent with real levels, two on one phone or each on their own in a team space, every move kept, and a look taken from the board and pieces.

npx skills add dodojo-AI/dodojo-skills --skill dodojo-game
示例Examples
1喂给它Give it
Make me a gomoku game I can play against the computer, or with a friend on one phone, and keep the game so we can finish it later.
输入Input
想玩什么、几个人、在哪儿玩；连上 dodojo 时，还有你以前做过的版本What to play, how many people and where; plus earlier versions in dodojo when connected
产出Output
一页打开就能玩的游戏：对局条、棋盘或场地、一排按钮，规则、设置和战绩放在下面A page you play: a status strip, the board or playfield and its controls, with rules, settings and records below
2它怎么做How it works
  1. 1
    先把游戏定下来Pin down the game谁玩、在哪儿玩、玩多久，规则和变体写清楚；要两个人的游戏加上分档的电脑对手who plays, where and for how long, the exact rules and variant, and a computer opponent if two people are needed.
  2. 2
    游戏放在最上面Lay it out with the game first细细一条对局条、游戏区、紧贴着的一排按钮，然后才是玩法、设置、战绩和规则a slim status strip, the play area, the controls under it, and only then how to play, settings, records and rules.
  3. 3
    从游戏的世界来设计Design from the game's world棋盘和棋子的材质、深浅两套配色，先写成方案再写代码the material of the board and pieces, a palette for light and dark, written as a plan before any code.
  4. 4
    做好输入和反馈Build input and feedback鼠标、触屏、键盘都能玩，反馈和事情大小相称，声音用 Web Audio 现做，动画从不让人等pointer, touch and keyboard, feedback in proportion to the moment, sound made with Web Audio, motion that never makes people wait.
  5. 5
    让人想再来一局Make another round tempting看得见的目标和纪录、公平的规则、难度曲线、像样的结局和复盘visible goals and records, fair play, a difficulty curve, an ending that is a moment and a way to look back.
  6. 6
    把棋局存住Keep the game每一步都存进页面状态，换台设备打开也停在同一手every move saved with the page's state, so it reopens on the same move on any device.
  7. 7
    下完一局再存Play it, then save dodojo每种模式用鼠标、触屏、键盘各下完整一局，手机宽度和深浅色都看过，再存成正文简短的 dodojo 笔记whole games in every mode with mouse, touch and keyboard, screenshots at phone width in both themes, then a dodojo Note with a short body.

步骤取自 SKILL.md 的 Workflow 一节，skill 改了，这里跟着变。金色步骤是和 dodojo 打交道的地方。Steps are read from the Workflow section of SKILL.md, so this page changes with the skill. Gold steps are where dodojo comes in.

Gomoku on a kaya board

Claude·11月26日Nov 26

Gomoku

Gomoku on a 15 × 15 board: play the computer (Easy, Medium or Hard) or a friend on the same device. Black moves first, five or more in a row wins with no forbidden moves, and the players swap colors after every game.

Every move is kept in this Note, so a game left halfway carries on when you open it again, on any of your devices. Your record and fastest wins are kept here too.

Mancala on a carved board

Claude·11月26日Nov 26

Mancala

Mancala with the Kalah rules, 6 pits a side and 4 seeds in each: play the computer (Easy, Medium or Hard) or a friend, on one phone or each on your own in a team space. Tap a pit on your side to sow its seeds counterclockwise.

Last seed in your store: go again. Last seed in an empty pit of yours: capture the seeds opposite. Most seeds in your store wins.
