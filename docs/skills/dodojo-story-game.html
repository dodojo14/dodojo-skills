dodojo-story-game互动故事Story game

分支剧情、推理案卷、密室逃脱、文字冒险，还有用你们共同回忆做的寻宝和生日惊喜：先画好故事地图再动笔，每段短、选择算数，谜题线索都在文字里，提示一级一级给，几种结局都要自己挣来。Branching stories, mystery case files, escape rooms, text adventures, and treasure hunts or birthday quests built from your shared memories: the story mapped before it is written, short scenes with real choices, fair clues in the text, a hint ladder and endings that are earned.

npx skills add dodojo-AI/dodojo-skills --skill dodojo-story-game
1喂给它Give it
Make me a short mystery game I can solve on my phone in one evening: a case file, clues, and a few possible endings.
输入Input
想要什么样的故事、谁来玩、玩多久；连上 dodojo 时，还有可以写进去的旅行、地点和照片The kind of story, who plays, how long; plus trips, places and photos in dodojo to write into it when connected
产出Output
一页一段一段读下去的故事：场景和文字在上面，选项紧跟着，线索本、结局图和设置放在下面A story you read scene by scene: the scene and its text on top, the choices right under it, with the journal, endings and settings below
2它怎么做How it works
  1. 1
    先把故事定下来Pin down the story哪一种、谁玩给谁玩、玩家的身份和目标、多长，要不要用你自己的回忆来写the kind, who plays and for whom, the player's role and goal, the length, and whether it is built from the person's own memories.
  2. 2
    动笔前先画地图Map it before writing it几幕、每个场景、每个选择改变什么、线索在哪找到、每种结局怎么挣来the acts, the scenes, the choices and what each changes, the clues and where they are found, and the endings each earned.
  3. 3
    写短场景和真选择Write short scenes with real choices每段 40 到 150 字，具体的处境，两到四个各有分量的选项forty to a hundred and fifty words a scene, a concrete situation, two to four choices that each mean something.
  4. 4
    谜题要公平Make the puzzles fair答案都能从页面上推出来，答案框容得下小笔误，提示从点拨到答案一级一级给every answer reasoned from what the page shows, an answer box that forgives small slips, a hint ladder from a nudge to the answer.
  5. 5
    场景放在最上面Lay it out with the scene first场景的图和文字在上，选项紧跟着，然后是线索本、结局图和设置the scene's picture and text on top, the choices right under it, then the journal, the map of endings and the settings.
  6. 6
    把故事存住Keep the story当前场景、做过的选择、找到的线索和到过的结局都存在页面里，可以从某一章重玩the scene, the choices, the clues found and the endings reached kept with the page; a chapter can be replayed.
  7. 7
    走遍每条路再存Walk every path, then save dodojo脚本走遍每个场景和结局，亲手从头玩一遍，手机宽度截图后存成 dodojo 笔记a script that visits every scene and ending, a full play-through by hand, screenshots at phone width, then a dodojo Note.

The Hollin Frost

A case-file mystery for one evening on a phone, about 30 to 40 minutes. You are the insurance adjuster sent to the Hollin Winter Garden in December 1956, the morning after a frost killed its famous orchid: find out who let it freeze, and why, before the Inspector's four o'clock.

Choose what to do under each scene; your notebook keeps every clue, and each lock has a hint ladder. Three endings to find.
