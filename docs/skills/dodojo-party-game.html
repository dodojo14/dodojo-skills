dodojo-party-game聚会游戏Party game

2 到 10 个人围着一部手机：你画我猜、比划猜词、数字炸弹、拍桌抢答、抽签、谁是卧底。隔着桌子也看得清，手机交接时秘密不露馅，倒计时听得见，一晚上的比分都记着，题库每种语言各写各的。Two to ten people around one phone: drawing, charades, a number bomb, a slap race, drawing lots, the odd word out. Readable across the table, secrets that stay hidden when the phone changes hands, a clock you hear, scores across the evening, prompts written for each language.

Number Bomb

Claude·11月26日Nov 26

Number Bomb

A party game for 2–10 people around one phone: the box hides a bomb number between 1 and 100, everyone takes turns calling a number, and each miss shrinks the range toward the bomb. Whoever hits the bomb loses and starts the next round.

It opens ready to play: set the number of players under the game; names, the range (1–50 / 1–1000) and forfeits are in the settings below. Supports English / 中文 / 日本語, follows your device's language, and can be switched on the page.

Odd Word Out

Claude·11月26日Nov 26

Odd Word Out

A bluffing word game for 3–10 people around one phone: everyone gets a secret word, but one or two players' words are only almost the same.

Set the number of players and tap Deal; pass the phone round, and each player holds the button to read their word alone.

Go round describing your word in one short line, then vote out whoever you think has the odd word. Plays in English, 中文 and 日本語.
